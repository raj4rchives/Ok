06 FAQ

Does the tracker save my progress?+

Yes. The tracker uses your browser's local storage, so your tasks remain after refreshing the page on the same browser/device.

Can I track Physics, Chemistry and Maths separately?+

Yes. Add each task under Physics, Chemistry, Maths or Other and see subject-wise progress automatically.

Can I use it for both Mains and Advanced?+

Absolutely. Use the daily checklist for lectures, DPPs, homework, PYQs, revision, tests and Advanced-level practice.

What should I add as daily tasks?+

Keep tasks specific: “Physics DPP 03”, “20 Maths PYQs”, “IOC revision”, “Chemistry lecture 4”, etc. Small measurable tasks are easier to complete.
